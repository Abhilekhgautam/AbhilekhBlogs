After careful consideration, I've left my role as a C++ Systems Engineer at Guardware Australia.

This decision is mainly based on what I want to do in the coming year.I want to spend more time pursuing my interests in programming languages and compilers, with the long-term goal of pursuing a career in academia.

I'll miss the people, the gossip, the fun, the banters and more importantly writing my beloved programming language C++.

I worked for nearly 1.5 years, mostly worked on Windows programming particularly Windows Shell Extension (Context Menu, Icon Handler, Icon Overlay), Micorsoft WebView2 and a bit of network programming while maintaining a legacy Proxy Codebase.

## The Best Part

  - Silly conversations
  - The banter
  - Interesting problems
  - Kind people
  - And, obviously, C++.

At last, I'll miss yapping **hailo sailo** after every few words, the random banters, and all those completely unnecessary walks across the office.
